Step 4 · Customise

/pulse-motion:style

Create your own video style (“a papercraft style in pop-art colours”): look, colours, fonts and movement, checked before use.

    Description

    Pulse Motion comes with six styles, and your brand kit (logo, colours, fonts) applies to them. If you want to go further, with a look that is yours alone, this command creates a personal style: its mood, its default colours, its fonts, its way of moving, and its nine ready-made scenes.

    The style is stored on your computer. It is then offered for all your videos, alongside the built-in styles.

    Why use it

    • Your videos look like no one else’s. Papercraft, retro poster, sketchbook: you describe the world, Pulse Motion turns it into a style.
    • A consistent series. All your videos share the same look, without redoing the work each time.
    • Checked before use. The style goes through the same checks as the built-in styles: readability, contrast, overflow in all four formats.

    • At any time, outside a video: the style belongs to no video folder.

    Tip: you only want your colours and your logo? No need for a style: choose « mon logo et mes couleurs » (my logo and my colours) during /pulse-motion:creer, or ask /pulse-motion:retoucher for « prends les couleurs de mon logo » (“use the colours of my logo”).

    How to run it

    /pulse-motion:style [le style voulu en une phrase]

    “[the style you want, in one sentence]”

    In square brackets [ ]: optional. Without a description, Pulse Motion asks you for one.

    /pulse-motion:style un style papercraft aux couleurs pop-art

    “a papercraft style in pop-art colours”

    A sentence works too: « crée mon propre style, sombre avec des touches néon » (“create my own style, dark with touches of neon”). To try one of the built-in styles or switch to another, that is an edit (/pulse-motion:retoucher).

    The interview

    One question at a time, each with suggested answers. Pulse Motion only asks what it cannot work out from your description.

    QuestionWhat you decide
    PersonalityThree words that describe the style, and what it must not suggest at any cost.
    ReferencesA work, a brand, a movement (« comme les affiches de Lichtenstein » (“like Lichtenstein’s posters”)).
    ColourLight background, dark, or both; understated with one accent, a bold colour, several colours, or colour everywhere.
    FontsTwo free families (Bunny Fonts), one for headings, one for text, checked before they are offered to you.
    MovementLively or gentle, with or without bounces.

    Pulse Motion then starts from the closest built-in style: its layout is taken over, then transformed. You can adapt a style you like, or start from a blank page (Pulse Motion then tells you which style it is starting from).

    Five changes at most per round (a font required by your brand kit does not count). Before writing, Pulse Motion shows you a summary: before, after, what it costs. Then a sheet opens: the starting style on the left, yours on the right, with the same little story (« la chaîne du froid » (“the cold chain”)).

    What it produces

    A style folder, stored in your personal folder: .pulse-motion/styles/<nom>/, in your user folder.

    FileWhat it is for
    identite.jsonThe style’s personality: what it suggests, what it avoids, its genres, its signature.
    charte.jsonThe default colours and fonts, replaced by your brand kit when you have one.
    style.cssThe look shared by all the scenes, in all four formats.
    scenes/The nine ready-made scenes: title, list, figure, comparison, step, alert, diagram, hub, conclusion.
    polices/The font files and their licences (LICENCES.md).
    guide.mdThe style’s guide, read by Pulse Motion to write your videos: the starting style’s guide, in 11 sections (what it is and isn’t, colour, movement, sound, gestures, pitfalls…), rewritten for yours.
    changements.jsonThe log of your changes, round by round: before, after, why.

    During creation, sheets (still images of the scenes) open in your browser, in 16:9 and vertical, with sample text and then at maximum length.

    Risks and precautions

    • The name must be new. It is written in lower case with hyphens (papercraft, affiche-retro) and cannot reuse the name of a built-in style.
    • Some effects are left out. Generated textures, layer blending and background blurs render badly at export: a paper texture goes through an image, a cut-out shadow through an offset shadow.
    • Nothing loops. A video must end on a steady picture: the style has no endless animation.
    • A modified style must be checked again. If you change a style file by hand, it is no longer offered until it has been checked again: ask « vérifie mon style papercraft » (“check my papercraft style”).
    • The starting style may change. After a Pulse Motion update, if the style yours started from has been improved, Pulse Motion tells you once and offers to bring in its fixes; nothing changes without your agreement.
    • The style stays on this computer. To use it elsewhere, copy its folder to the same place on the other computer.

    Problems and solutions

    “Le style est prêt, mais son affichage n'a pas pu être contrôlé” (The style is ready, but its display could not be checked)

    Neither Chrome nor Edge was found: overflow was not checked on screen. The style can still be used. Install one of the two browsers (see the requirements) and ask « vérifie mon style papercraft » (“check my papercraft style”).

    I don’t like the sample scenes

    Answer « Je veux changer quelque chose » (I want to change something) and say what, in your own words: « plus de couleur », « des coins moins ronds », « les titres moins gros » (“more colour”, “less rounded corners”, “smaller headings”). Pulse Motion fixes it and redoes the sheet, until you are happy.

    The font I wanted is not offered

    Pulse Motion offers free fonts from Bunny Fonts, which it checks before showing them to you. Give the exact name of yours: if it isn’t there, it suggests the closest ones.
