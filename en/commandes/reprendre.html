/pulse-motion:reprendre

Pick up the current video where it stopped.

    Description

    Closed VS Code halfway through a video? A conversation got interrupted? This command reads the state of the video in the folder again, tells you in one sentence where it stands, then carries on at exactly the right step.

    It also reads your personal glossary (in French): tool words that have already been explained to you aren’t explained again.

    Where the video wasWhat Pulse Motion does
    No video in the folderSuggests /pulse-motion:creer.
    The timeline has errorsFixes them, then shows you the timeline.
    The timeline is waiting for your approvalShows it to you and asks for your approval.
    Scenes are missing, or are still only draftsOpens the preview on the animated draft if it is closed, has the animators write the scenes, then checks the video.
    The style is waiting for your approval (guided path)Shows you the style comparison again.
    The final video is waiting for your approval (guided path)Opens the preview and asks for your approval.
    The video is readyReopens the preview if it is closed, then suggests an edit.

    Why use it

    • Nothing is lost. The whole state of the video is in its folder: you can shut the computer down at any step.
    • You don’t have to remember. Pulse Motion knows which step comes next and offers it to you.
    • Your brand kit stays up to date. If the saved brand kit used by this video has changed since (another colour, a new logo), Pulse Motion points it out and offers to apply it again. Nothing changes without your approval.

    • After a break, when you reopen a video’s folder.
    • When Pulse Motion reminds you. At the start of each session, it checks whether the folder holds a video in progress and offers to pick it up.
    • After restarting Claude Code, for example to finish installing transcription.
    • To find your videos: « quelles sont mes vidéos ? » (“what are my videos?”), « retrouve ma vidéo sur la chaîne du froid » (“find my video about the cold chain”). Claude shows you the list of your videos, wherever they are on the computer: where each one stands, its length, its style and its MP4. To edit one, open Claude Code in its folder.

    How to run it

    /pulse-motion:reprendre

    It takes nothing after the name. Run it in the video’s folder: in VS Code, File menu → Open Folder…, then open the Claude Code panel.

    What it produces

    Nothing new on its own: it picks up the work of /pulse-motion:creer where it stopped, and so produces the same files (timeline, scenes, animated page in sortie/).

    Risks and precautions

    • It changes nothing before talking to you. It first tells you where the video stands.
    • One folder, one video. Make sure you open the video’s folder, not the folder that contains it: otherwise Pulse Motion finds no video.

    Problems and solutions

    Pulse Motion says there is no video here

    The folder you opened isn’t the video’s folder. It must contain a deroule.json file. Open the right folder with File → Open Folder…, then run the command again.

    My saved brand kit no longer exists

    It has been deleted from your computer. The video keeps its own copy of the brand kit, in its charte/ folder: nothing is broken. Pulse Motion simply lets you know.

    pulse-motion etat .

    La vidéo « La chaîne du froid » attend encore votre accord sur son déroulé. Le voici : 6 scènes, 1 min 03. → Je valide, avec le style posé, lisible, rassurant (Recommandé) / Je veux changer quelque chose

    In English

    You: /pulse-motion:reprendre

    Claude: The video “La chaîne du froid” is still waiting for your approval of its timeline. Here it is: 6 scenes, 1 min 03 s. → I approve, with the calm, readable, reassuring style (Recommended) / I want to change something

    /pulse-motion:reprendre

    La vidéo « La chaîne du froid » est prête. J'ai rouvert l'aperçu dans votre navigateur. Une retouche ? Décrivez-la en une phrase, par exemple « ralentis la scène 3 ».

    In English

    You: /pulse-motion:reprendre

    Claude: The video “La chaîne du froid” is ready. I’ve reopened the preview in your browser. An edit? Describe it in one sentence, for example “slow down scene 3”.
