/pulse-motion:voix

Record, import and align the video’s voice, or play its music (« j'enregistre la scène 2 » (“I’m recording scene 2”), « j'ai un fichier de ma voix » (“I have a file of my voice”), « recale les mots » (“realign the words”)).

    Description

    This is the command for your video’s voice, scene by scene. It can do five things:

    • Record your voice in the preview’s studio. The scene’s text is shown in large type; you click « Enregistrer » (Record), read after the countdown, then « Arrêter » (Stop). You listen back, then « Garder » (Keep) or « Refaire » (Redo).
    • Import a recording made elsewhere, for one scene or for the whole video. A recording of the whole video is split up by scene.
    • Add a placeholder voice: the computer’s voice reads the text, to time the animation while you wait to record.
    • Add a synthetic voice: a French voice generated on your computer, with no account and no connection once installed. It can be delivered as it is, with its credits.
    • Realign the words with what you actually said, thanks to transcription (in French).

    It can also play the music on its own, in your computer’s media player.

    The scenes to be spoken are the ones with spoken text in the timeline. A video with no spoken text at all has nothing to record: Pulse Motion then offers to add some with /pulse-motion:retoucher (« ajoute une voix à la vidéo » (“add a voice to the video”)).

    This command doesn’t change the volumes, the spoken text, the subtitles or the mood of the music: for that, use /pulse-motion:retoucher (« baisse la voix » (“turn the voice down”), « change ce que je dis dans l'intro » (“change what I say in the intro”)).

    Why use it

    • No recording software to learn. The studio is in the preview: the text to read is right in front of you.
    • A recording made elsewhere works too. Pulse Motion reads it where it is and stores the take with the video: the original doesn’t move.
    • The video fits itself to your voice. A scene lasts at least as long as its take, and an animation that starts on a word of the voice follows that word. Re-recording a scene realigns it automatically.
    • You can move forward before recording. The placeholder voice holds the place; your real takes are always kept.

    How to run it

    /pulse-motion:voix

    It needs nothing after the name: say what you want in your own words, right after the name or in the conversation.

    /pulse-motion:voix j'enregistre la scène 2

    “I’m recording scene 2”

    /pulse-motion:voix j'ai un fichier de ma voix

    “I have a file of my voice”

    What you can ask

    You sayWhat happens
    « j'enregistre la scène 2 » (“I’m recording scene 2”), « je veux enregistrer ma voix » (“I want to record my voice”)The preview opens on the studio for the scene you asked for (otherwise, the first one to record). The first time, the browser asks you to allow the microphone.
    « j'ai un fichier de ma voix » (“I have a file of my voice”), « importe mon enregistrement » (“import my recording”)Pulse Motion asks you for the file (its path, or drag it into the conversation), then: “for one scene or for the whole video?”. It recommends the whole video if the recording is longer than a minute.
    « mets une voix provisoire » (“add a placeholder voice”), « je veux entendre le texte » (“I want to hear the text”)The computer’s voice reads the text of the whole video, or of a single scene. Takes already recorded are kept.
    « mets une voix de synthèse » (“add a synthetic voice”)Pulse Motion generates the voice for each scene that doesn’t have your take. The first time, it offers to install it (about 300 MB, a few minutes, with your consent); meanwhile, the computer’s voice holds the place. After that, only the scenes that changed are remade.
    « fais-la parler plus lentement » (“make it speak more slowly”), « un peu plus vite » (“a bit faster”)The speed of the synthetic voice changes (from 0.7 to 1.3 times), then the scenes are remade.
    « il prononce mal HACCP » (“it mispronounces HACCP”)Pulse Motion offers to note the correct pronunciation in the glossary, for your next videos too, or only in this video. If transcription is installed, it listens back to the synthetic voice itself and tells you which words were misread.
    « recale les mots » (“realign the words”), « la voix est décalée » (“the voice is out of sync”), « les sous-titres sont en avance » (“the subtitles are early”)Transcription realigns the words of your takes. If it isn’t installed, Pulse Motion offers to install it first.
    « fais-moi écouter la musique » (“let me hear the music”)The music on its own opens in your computer’s media player. With no music, Pulse Motion offers to add some with /pulse-motion:retoucher.

    The voice, subtitles and music are covered in detail on the Le son page (in French).

    What it produces

    • Your voice takes, one per scene, stored with the video in assets/voix/. Alongside each take, Pulse Motion keeps the moment each word is said.
    • The updated video: sortie/<titre>.html, and the preview, which reloads by itself.
    • A check after each recording, import or placeholder voice. Pulse Motion tells you in one sentence what remains: scenes still using the placeholder voice, a scene made longer by the voice, words aligned as well as possible.
    • The music on its own, when you ask to hear it: sortie/musique.wav.

    Risks and precautions

    • The voice sets the length of the scenes. A scene lasts at least as long as its take, plus a short silence. A take longer than planned therefore makes the scene longer: Pulse Motion tells you.
    • The synthetic voice is honest, not human. A single French voice, female, decent but less natural than a real voice; acronyms, proper names and English words are corrected through the glossary. It doesn’t imitate anyone. Your take always comes before it.
    • The placeholder voice is never delivered. It is only used to time the animation. The export refuses it, unless you ask for a working version yourself.
    • A recording of the whole video needs transcription. That is what splits it up by scene. It is installed once, with your consent (about 800 MB, see /pulse-motion:outils). Without it, import one take per scene.
    • The subtitles use the written text, never what transcription thought it heard. If you said something other than the text, change the spoken text with /pulse-motion:retoucher.

    Tip: to redo a single scene, give its number (« j'enregistre la scène 3 » (“I’m recording scene 3”)): only its take is replaced.

    Problems and solutions

    Pulse Motion says there is no video here

    The folder you opened doesn’t contain a video. Open your video’s folder in VS Code, or create one with /pulse-motion:creer.

    No scene has any text to speak

    The video was created without a voice. Ask « ajoute une voix à la vidéo » (“add a voice to the video”) with /pulse-motion:retoucher: Pulse Motion writes the spoken text for each scene. You can then record it.

    Importing the whole video is refused

    Transcription isn’t installed, and it is what splits the recording up by scene. Pulse Motion offers to install it, then runs the same import again. If you’d rather not install it, import or record one take per scene.

    Pulse Motion reports a poorly recognised scene

    After importing the whole video, fewer than half the words in this scene were recognised: its take may have been split badly. Re-record this scene in the studio (« j'enregistre la scène 4 » (“I’m recording scene 4”)), or import a take just for it.

    The studio doesn’t record my voice

    The browser asks for permission to use the microphone the first time. If you refused, allow the microphone for the preview page in the browser’s settings, then ask « j'enregistre la scène 1 » (“I’m recording scene 1”) again.

    The subtitles are ahead of or behind the voice

    The words were aligned as well as possible, without transcription. Ask « recale les mots » (“realign the words”): Pulse Motion realigns them with your voice, after installing transcription if needed.

    “node” not found

    Node.js isn’t installed. Install the LTS version from nodejs.org, then restart Claude Code. See the requirements.
