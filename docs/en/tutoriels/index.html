Learn by doing

The tutorials

Two training videos, a film with characters, then a screen-recorded software tutorial, from the first message to the MP4 file. You type a command, Pulse Motion asks you questions, and you answer in your own words. Before you start, check the requirements.

  • Tutorial 1 · About 30 minutesFirst video“The cold chain”, a one-minute training video on the quick path: the timeline, the style, the preview, one edit, then the MP4. With composed music and no voice.
  • Tutorial 2 · About 1 h 30 minFull video (in French)“The 5 kitchen hygiene habits”, on the guided path: your logo and your colours, your voice recorded in the studio, subtitles, sound effects, edits, then the high-fidelity export.
  • Tutorial 3 · Advanced · About 5 hoursRecreating “Vibe Connection” (in French)A mock silent film, 1 min 40 s long, in square format for LinkedIn: animated characters acting out a comedy, seven composed sequences, a camera, a freeze frame, particle effects, music timed to the picture and original sound effects.
  • Tutorial 4 · About 40 minutesFilming a piece of software (in French)“Creating an invoice in Facturo”, a 30-second tutorial filmed on the demo app: describe the journey, sign in, blur, pick your take, know what stays on your computer, and import a recording of your screen.

Which one to choose?

In order

Tutorial 1 walks you through the whole journey, quickly. Tutorial 2 goes through each step in depth and adds everything that makes a video truly yours. Tutorial 3 assumes you have done the first two: it builds a whole film, with characters, sequence by sequence. Tutorial 4, shorter, comes after the first one: it films an application.
