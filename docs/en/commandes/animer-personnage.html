Step 4 · Customise

/pulse-motion:animer-personnage

Create or change a character’s pose or move, and see it from several angles (« fais une pose où il se gratte la tête » (“make a pose where he scratches his head”), « il marche trop vite » (“he walks too fast”)).

    Description

    A Pulse Motion character moves with poses (a held attitude: pointing, hands on hips, sitting) and moves (walking, running, a jump, a gesture). It also changes expression: joy, sadness, fear, anger, surprise. This command creates new ones or changes existing ones, for a drawn character as well as for the stick figure.

    After each change, Pulse Motion checks the result and looks at it on a sheet, an image made of thumbnails that shows the move from several angles: limbs must not bend the wrong way, feet must stay on the ground, the gesture must look like what you asked for. It fixes things, in three rounds at most, then shows you the sheet.

    It is also used to put a character in a scene and have it act there: walk in, point at a figure, wave, leave, or talk over the scene’s voice.

    Why use it

    • A new gesture in one sentence. « Fais une pose où il se gratte la tête » (“Make a pose where he scratches his head”), « fais-le sauter plus haut » (“make him jump higher”).
    • You see it from every angle: in profile, three-quarter, from the front.
    • The built-in moves stay intact. To change the walk, Pulse Motion makes a copy stored with your video, which replaces the walk for that video only.
    • The character acts with the scene. Its actions are timed to the other elements or to a word in the voice; between two actions it breathes, and every change happens smoothly.

    How to run it

    /pulse-motion:animer-personnage [la pose ou le mouvement voulu]

    “[the pose or move you want]”

    In square brackets [ ]: optional. You can also run the command on its own and describe the gesture afterwards, in your own words.

    /pulse-motion:animer-personnage fais une pose où il se gratte la tête

    “make a pose where he scratches his head”

    /pulse-motion:animer-personnage montre-moi la course de face et de profil

    “show me the run from the front and in profile”

    /pulse-motion:animer-personnage mets le cuisinier dans la scène 3, qu'il montre le chiffre

    “put the chef in scene 3, pointing at the figure”

    ActionWhat it does

    Strike a poseHold an attitude for a while.
    Play a moveA jump, a wave…

    TalkThe mouth follows the words of the scene’s voice.

    What it produces

    • The new or changed poses and moves, stored with the video: personnages/poses/<nom>.json and personnages/clips/<nom>.json.
    • A sheet showing the move from several angles, with a sentence about what has changed.
    • For a new pose or move, a question: « Je le garde pour vos prochaines vidéos ? » (Shall I keep it for your next videos?). Once kept, it joins your personal library.
    • For a character in a scene: the updated scene, the video checked, and a sheet of the scene to check its position.

    Risks and precautions

    • Creating needs a video in the folder. Without one, Pulse Motion can only show you the moves; it offers to create a video with /pulse-motion:creer.
    • A change only applies to this video. The built-in moves are never changed: your slower walk stays specific to the video in the folder.
    • To talk, the scene must have a voice. If two characters share the voice, each one talks during its part, which starts on a word.
    • A gesture during a walk would make the character slide. Pulse Motion places poses and gestures after the end of the journey.
    • Text stays readable. Pulse Motion places it out of the character’s path, or has the character cross the scene before the text arrives.

    Problems and solutions

    Pulse Motion says it needs a video’s folder

    You can look at the moves anywhere, but a new pose or move is stored with a video. Open your video’s folder, or create one with /pulse-motion:creer.

    The move doesn’t look like what I wanted

    Say what is wrong, in one sentence: « il lève le bras trop haut », « plus lentement » (“he raises his arm too high”, “more slowly”). To set the position of the arms and legs yourself, open /pulse-motion:atelier-personnages.

    The character doesn’t talk

    The scene has no voice. Add one with /pulse-motion:retoucher (« ajoute une voix à la vidéo » (“add a voice to the video”)), or record it with /pulse-motion:voix.

    Its mouth moves badly when it talks

    A drawn character needs speaking mouths. Ask for them with /pulse-motion:dessiner-personnage (« dessine-lui les bouches pour parler » (“draw him the mouths for talking”)).

    My changed walk doesn’t appear in another video

    That is intended: a change is stored with the video where you made it. Make the request again in the other video.

    “node” not found

    Node.js is not installed. Install the LTS version from nodejs.org, then restart Claude Code. See the requirements.

    Examples

    /pulse-motion:animer-personnage il marche trop vite

    pulse-motion personnages afficher marche

    pulse-motion personnages verifier --dossier .

    pulse-motion personnages planche marche --vues 0,45,90 --dessin cuisinier --dossier .

    Le cuisinier marche plus posément : chaque pas dure un peu plus longtemps. Ce changement vaut pour cette vidéo seulement. Pour régler la marche vous-même, à la souris : /pulse-motion:atelier-personnages.

    In English

    You: /pulse-motion:animer-personnage he walks too fast

    Claude: The chef now walks more calmly: each step lasts a little longer. This change applies to this video only. To adjust the walk yourself, with the mouse: /pulse-motion:atelier-personnages.

    /pulse-motion:animer-personnage mets le cuisinier dans la scène 3, qu'il entre et montre le chiffre

    pulse-motion assembler .

    pulse-motion controler .

    pulse-motion planche . chiffre

    Dans la scène 3, le cuisinier entre en marchant par la gauche, s'arrête, puis montre le chiffre quand il apparaît. Le texte reste dégagé. L'aperçu s'est rechargé.

    In English

    You: /pulse-motion:animer-personnage put the chef in scene 3, have him come in and point at the figure

    Claude: In scene 3, the chef walks in from the left, stops, then points at the figure when it appears. The text stays clear. The preview has reloaded.

    /pulse-motion:animer-personnage fais-le parler dans l'intro

    pulse-motion assembler .

    pulse-motion planche . intro

    Dans l'intro, la bouche du cuisinier suit maintenant les mots de la voix, jusqu'à la fin de la scène.

    In English

    You: /pulse-motion:animer-personnage make him talk in the intro

    Claude: In the intro, the chef’s mouth now follows the words of the voice, until the end of the scene.

    Related commands

    • /pulse-motion:dessiner-personnage: draw the character, or edit its drawing.
    • /pulse-motion:atelier-personnages: adjust a pose or a move with the mouse.
    • /pulse-motion:voix: record the voice it talks over.
