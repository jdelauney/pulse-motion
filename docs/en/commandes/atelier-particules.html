Step 4 · Customise

/pulse-motion:atelier-particules

Open the particle effects workshop to adjust an effect while watching it (« laisse-moi régler les étincelles » (“let me adjust the sparks”), « je veux voir ce que donne la neige » (“I want to see what the snow looks like”)).

    Description

    Particle effects (sparks, confetti, snow, smoke…) are usually edited in one sentence: « plus d'étincelles » (“more sparks”), « en bleu » (“in blue”). This command lets you adjust them by eye, with sliders, while watching the result. Depending on your request, Pulse Motion opens one of these two places:

    • The preview’s « Effets » (Effects) button, for an effect already placed in a scene. You choose the effect from the list, or click on it in the picture; the sliders change the picture straight away, and playback loops on the effect. « Enregistrer » (Save) stores your settings in the scene. « Recette complète » (Full recipe) shows all the effect’s settings, and « Enregistrer comme nouvel effet » (Save as new effect) turns it into an effect of your own, which the scene uses at once.
    • The workshop page, to work on an effect on its own (« laisse-moi régler les étincelles » (“let me adjust the sparks”)). The effect loops, on a light background (your brand kit’s) or a dark one; you move and resize with the mouse the area where the particles appear. « Enregistrer » stores the effect with your video: your settings become its starting settings.

    When you say you have finished, Pulse Motion reads the chosen setting and describes it to you in one sentence.

    Why use it

    • An effect is judged by eye. The right amount of snow or the right speed for a burst is found faster by watching it.
    • The result is immediate, without reloading the page.
    • You can create your own effect from a built-in one, and reuse it in the video.
    • Nothing is lost. Every save goes through the history: « annule » (“undo”) reverses it.

    • After /pulse-motion:creer, when a scene has an effect you want to fine-tune.

    • Instead of /pulse-motion:retoucher when a sentence is no longer enough. For a simple change (« plus d'étincelles » (“more sparks”), « enlève les confettis » (“remove the confetti”)), an edit is quicker.

    How to run it

    /pulse-motion:atelier-particules [l'effet à régler]

    “[the effect to adjust]”

    In square brackets [ ]: optional. You can also run the command on its own and then say which effect to adjust.

    /pulse-motion:atelier-particules laisse-moi régler les étincelles

    “let me adjust the sparks”

    /pulse-motion:atelier-particules je veux voir ce que donne la neige

    “I want to see what the snow looks like”

    The built-in effects: étincelles (sparks), braises (embers), confettis (confetti), feu d’artifice (fireworks), fumée (smoke), feu (fire), poussière (dust), neige (snow), pluie (rain), paillettes (glitter), énergie (energy), explosion (explosion). They are described in the catalogue.

    You only say « ouvre l'éditeur » (“open the editor”), and your video also has characters? Pulse Motion asks you which: « Les effets de particules » (The particle effects) or « Les personnages » (The characters). For characters, use /pulse-motion:atelier-personnages.

    What it produces

    • The effect’s settings, stored in the scene, when you save from the preview’s « Effets » button.
    • An effect of your own, stored with the video in effets/<nom>.json, when you save a new effect or an effect adjusted on the workshop page.
    • A sentence summing up the chosen setting, once you have finished.

    Risks and precautions

    • Outside a video’s folder, the workshop does not save. It opens anyway so you can try things out; Pulse Motion warns you.
    • A scene changed in the meantime is not overwritten. If the scene changed while you were adjusting (an edit, for example), the workshop refuses to save and offers to reload the page.
    • The name of a new effect is written in lower case, with numbers and hyphens, and must differ from those of the built-in effects.

    Tip: an effect serves a purpose: highlighting a figure, celebrating a conclusion, setting a mood. One well-placed subtle effect is worth more than three spectacular ones.

    Problems and solutions

    I saved, but nothing changed in my video

    The open folder probably has no video in it: the workshop adjusts, but does not save. Open your video’s folder in VS Code and run the command again.

    The workshop refuses to save and offers to reload the page

    The scene was changed while you were adjusting the effect. Reload the page, redo your adjustment, then save: nothing from the edit has been overwritten.

    The snow can’t be seen on a light background

    Snow and dust are white to start with. On a light background, choose your brand kit’s primary colour, or a blue-grey.

    Pulse Motion asks me which editor to open

    Your video has effects and characters, and “the editor” could mean either. Choose « Les effets de particules » (The particle effects); for characters, use /pulse-motion:atelier-personnages.

    “node” not found

    Node.js is not installed. Install the LTS version from nodejs.org, then restart Claude Code. See the requirements.

    Examples

    /pulse-motion:atelier-particules laisse-moi régler les étincelles de la scène 3

    pulse-motion apercu . --ouvrir

    L'aperçu est ouvert. Cliquez sur « Effets », puis choisissez les étincelles : les curseurs changent l'image tout de suite, et la lecture boucle sur l'effet. Cliquez sur « Enregistrer » quand elles vous plaisent.

    C'est fait.

    Les étincelles de la scène 3 sont enregistrées : plus nombreuses et un peu plus lentes, dans la couleur d'accent de votre charte.

    In English

    You: /pulse-motion:atelier-particules let me adjust the sparks in scene 3

    Claude: The preview is open. Click “Effets” (Effects), then choose the sparks: the sliders change the picture straight away, and playback loops on the effect. Click “Enregistrer” (Save) when you like them.

    You: Done.

    Claude: The sparks in scene 3 are saved: more of them and a little slower, in your brand kit’s accent colour.

    /pulse-motion:atelier-particules je veux voir ce que donne la neige

    pulse-motion atelier neige

    L'atelier est ouvert sur la neige, qui tombe en boucle. Passez du fond clair au fond sombre, déplacez à la souris la zone d'où tombent les flocons, réglez les curseurs. « Enregistrer » range l'effet avec votre vidéo.

    In English

    You: /pulse-motion:atelier-particules I want to see what the snow looks like

    Claude: The workshop is open on the snow, which falls in a loop. Switch between the light and dark backgrounds, move the area the flakes fall from with the mouse, adjust the sliders. “Enregistrer” (Save) stores the effect with your video.
