Step 1 · Create

/pulse-motion:creer

Create a motion design video in a few questions, approve its timeline, then watch it in your browser.

    Description

    This is the command you start with. You give the subject in one sentence; Pulse Motion asks you two short rounds of questions, writes the timeline (the list of scenes, their text, their length) and shows it to you as a table. Nothing is animated until you approve it; as soon as you do, a first animated draft opens in your browser, in under a minute.

    Once the timeline is approved, Pulse Motion makes the video: a specialist assistant, the animator, writes each scene, all at the same time. Each finished scene replaces its draft in the preview, right before your eyes. Automatic checks then test readability and fix the safe cases on their own: a video that still contains a draft cannot be exported.

    One video = one folder. Run the command in an empty folder, open in VS Code. If the folder already holds a video, Pulse Motion offers to pick it up, or to create the new one somewhere else.

    Why use it

    • You talk about your subject, not about technique. Pulse Motion works out the genre (training, product, storytelling, social media) and the format, then picks the structure that works for that genre.
    • One approval is enough. On the quick path, you only approve the timeline, along with the style. Everything else is done for you.
    • The pace is right from the start. Each scene lasts long enough to be read (1.5 s + 0.3 s per word), nothing stays still for more than 2.5 s, and the video ends on a steady frame.
    • You see the video move straight away. An animated draft opens as soon as you approve the timeline, with the music; it is replaced scene by scene.
    • You compare before you choose. Three styles appear side by side, on your first two scenes, in your browser.

    How to run it

    /pulse-motion:creer [le sujet de la vidéo en une phrase]

    “[the subject of the video in one sentence]”

    In square brackets [ ]: optional. With no subject, there is a single question: « Que voulez-vous faire ? » (“What would you like to do?”): a video from your idea (recommended), start from a video template, see an example first, or a video in one of your styles (« Créer mon propre style » (“Create my own style”) if you don’t have one yet).

    /pulse-motion:creer la chaîne du froid en une minute pour mes stagiaires

    “the cold chain in one minute, for my trainees”

    /pulse-motion:creer présenter notre application de réservation en 30 secondes

    “present our booking app in 30 seconds”

    A plain sentence works too: « fais-moi une vidéo d'une minute sur la chaîne du froid » (“make me a one-minute video about the cold chain”), « que sait faire Pulse Motion ? » (“what can Pulse Motion do?”). Pulse Motion only creates a video if you ask for one, first reminds you that this is a new video in this folder, and writes nothing before your first answer; in a folder that already has a video, it offers to pick it up.

    The questions asked

    A first round, four questions at most:

    QuestionSuggested answers
    Length30 s · 1 min (recommended for a short training video) · 3 min · 5 min
    AudienceGeneral public · professionals in the field · pupils or trainees (adapted to your subject). Not asked if your subject already says so (« pour mes stagiaires » (“for my trainees”)).
    PathQuick (recommended): you only approve the timeline · Guided: you also approve the style and the final video · Do it for me: no more questions, you just watch the result
    To addMy logo and my colours · my company’s website (colours, logo and fonts suggested, after your consent) · « Ma charte… » (“My brand kit…”) if you have saved one · nothing for now

    Then a round on sound (in French):

    QuestionSuggested answers
    MusicMusic composed for the video (recommended) · I have a file · No music
    Voice and subtitlesMy voice, with subtitles (recommended) · My voice, without subtitles · Synthetic voice · No voice (so no subtitles: they repeat the spoken text). A file of your voice: just say so.
    The computerOnly if a useful tool is missing (voice transcription, ffmpeg): Get the computer ready while I work (recommended) · Later

    In total, without a brand kit: 7 answers at most on the quick path, 9 on the guided path.

    The “Do it for me” path

    You answer the first round, and that’s it: 4 answers. Pulse Motion chooses the rest for you: music composed to suit the genre, no voice and no subtitles (you can add them later), the recommended style, the words of your trade noted in the glossary, nothing installed. Only exception: it asks you for your logo file if you chose “My logo and my colours”. At the end, the summary lists « Mes choix » (“My choices”): each one can be changed in one sentence with /pulse-motion:retoucher (« passe en style éditorial » (“switch to the editorial style”)).

    The format follows from the genre: 16:9 (landscape) in general, 9:16 (vertical) for social media, 4:5 if you are aiming at the LinkedIn or Instagram feed.

    Starting from a template

    Pulse Motion recognises nine kinds of video from your subject. When it recognises one, it starts from its template: a proven scene structure, a length, a format, a musical mood and three styles that suit it. It tells you so in one sentence (« Je pars du modèle « Pitch » : 6 scènes, environ 1 min. » (“I’m starting from the ‘Pitch’ template: 6 scenes, about 1 min.”)) and opens the finished example of that template while you answer the questions, without writing anything in your folder; you can change everything afterwards. For a subject that matches no template, it carries on as usual.

    “the pitch for our reuse-centre project, from the pitch template”

    To see the result before you start, run the command below in an empty folder: the template’s finished example is copied there and opens in the preview.

    “show me an example of a pitch”

    All the ideas, with their video examples and sentences to copy, are on the Video ideas page.

    What it produces

    Everything is stored in the video’s folder. You never have to open these files, but you can read them.

    FileWhat it is for
    deroule.jsonThe timeline: the title, the genre, the format, the style, the music, and for each scene its text, its spoken text, its length and its transition.
    scenes/01-intro.html…One scene per file, numbered in order. The text isn’t written in them: it comes from the timeline.
    charte/Your logo, your colours (charte.json), your fonts and the glossary of your trade.
    assets/Your files: music, images, voice takes (assets/voix/).
    sortie/<titre>.htmlThe animated video, as a single page that opens with no internet connection.
    sortie/<titre>.srt and .vttThe subtitles, if you asked for them, for platforms that show them separately.
    sortie/controle.jsonThe report of the last check: what was fixed automatically, what remains.
    sortie/comparaison/The images from the style comparison.

    At the end, if you added your logo and your colours, Pulse Motion offers to save them as a brand kit (“client-dupont”, for example): it will be offered for your next videos.

    Risks and precautions

    • Nothing is animated before you approve. If the timeline doesn’t suit you, say what needs changing: Pulse Motion corrects it and shows it to you again.
    • Your files are copied, never moved. The logo, music or voice you provide is copied into the video’s folder; the original stays where it was.
    • Installations need your consent. Transcription (Python, about 800 MB) is only installed if you accept. Everything works without it.
    • The placeholder voice isn’t yours. If you record yourself, the computer’s voice first reads the text to time the animation. It is never delivered: export refuses it until you have recorded.

    Warning: a JPEG or WebP logo isn’t enough to pick out your colours cleanly. Provide a PNG or an SVG.

    Problems and solutions

    “node” not found, or Node.js too old

    Install the LTS version of Node.js from nodejs.org, then close VS Code completely and reopen it. See the requirements.

    Pulse Motion says a video already exists here

    The folder already holds a timeline. To carry on with that video: /pulse-motion:reprendre. To make a new one: create another empty folder, open it, and run the command again.

    The style comparison doesn’t open

    It opens in Chrome or Edge. Without them, Pulse Motion describes each style by its personality, in three words: choose on that basis. See Chrome or Edge.

    Pulse Motion tells me it made a scene longer

    That’s the reading check: the scene was too short to read its text. Pulse Motion lengthened it (by 50% at most) and tells you in one sentence. If you prefer a precise length, ask for it with /pulse-motion:retoucher.

    « Le rendu n'a pas pu être vérifié » (“The render could not be checked”)

    Neither Chrome nor Edge was found, or the page ran into an error. The video is made, but overflow and contrast haven’t been checked on screen. Install one of the two browsers, then ask for an edit, even a tiny one: the full check runs again.

    My font wasn’t found

    Pulse Motion fetches free fonts from Bunny Fonts. If yours isn’t there, it keeps the closest of the style’s fonts and tells you. You can give it the font file (.ttf, .otf, .woff or .woff2).
