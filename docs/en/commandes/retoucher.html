Step 2 · Edit

/pulse-motion:retoucher

Edit the video in one sentence (« ralentis la scène 3 » (“slow down scene 3”), « change le titre » (“change the title”), « annule » (“undo”)) and see the result straight away.

    Description

    You’re watching the video in the preview and something isn’t right: say so in one sentence. Pulse Motion makes the smallest change that answers your sentence, without “improving” anything else along the way. It checks the video, then the preview reloads by itself, at the same position.

    Every edit is saved before it is made. If you don’t like the result, « annule » (“undo”) goes back to the previous state.

    Why use it

    • You talk as you would to a video editor. No software to learn: « le chiffre doit rebondir » (“the number should bounce”), « mets la liste à droite » (“put the list on the right”).
    • You see the result within a second. The preview stays open and reloads at the same position.
    • You can try anything. « annule » (“undo”) goes back, « rétablis » (“redo”) reverses the undo; the last 20 edits are kept, and a version can be saved under a name (« envoyée au client » (“sent to the client”)) so you can get it back whenever you like.
    • You show instead of describing. In the preview, click « Remarque » (Note), then on the image where you want: the note is kept with the exact moment. Claude deals with them one by one and only marks a note as done after looking at the new image.
    • Your impressions become choices. « ça fait cheap » (“it looks cheap”), « plus premium » (“more premium”): Pulse Motion suggests two to four options, in plain words, and applies nothing without your answer.
    • An ambiguous sentence leads to a question, not a mistake. « Ralentis » (“Slow down”)? Pulse Motion asks you which scene, with the most likely answer first.

    • For your lasting preferences: « jamais de rebond » (“never any bounce”), « toujours mon logo à la fin » (“always my logo at the end”). What you ask for right now always comes first.
    • To keep an effect or a character: « garde cet effet de neige pour mes prochaines vidéos » (“keep this snow effect for my next videos”), « garde ce personnage » (“keep this character”). It joins your personal library; another video uses it by copying it, without ever changing this one.
    • To use your website’s colours: « prends les couleurs de notre site » (“use our website’s colours”). With your consent, Claude reads the home page and suggests two readable colours, the logo and the site’s fonts; nothing is changed before you answer, and no font file from the site is copied.

    How to run it

    /pulse-motion:retoucher [la retouche en une phrase]

    “[the edit in one sentence]”

    In square brackets [ ]: optional. With no sentence, Pulse Motion asks you what to change.

    /pulse-motion:retoucher ralentis la scène 3

    “slow down scene 3”

    /pulse-motion:retoucher annule

    “undo”

    In the preview

    • « Remarque » (Note) (or the N key): a click on the image pauses it and drops a pin; write your note, then « Garder la remarque » (Keep the note), « Sur cette image, sans épingle » (On this image, without a pin) or « Sur toute la vidéo » (On the whole video). Notes appear in colour on the playback bar (orange: to do, green: done). When you have finished: « Envoyer à Claude » (Send to Claude). A note that has been dealt with shows the before and after images side by side.
    • « Réglages » (Settings): accent colour, pace (calmer, normal, livelier), tone (light, dark, automatic), music (quieter, normal, louder, none) and subtitles (yes, no, word by word). The highlighted value is the video’s; a click writes it straight away, and « annule » (“undo”) reverses it.
    • « Comparer » (Compare): choose a saved version; both are shown side by side, driven by a single playback bar.
    • The playback bar shows the shape of the voice (hover over it to see the words, click to jump there) and, when you hover over the start of a scene, a small image of the scene with its name.

    None of this appears in the delivered video or in the MP4.

    What it produces

    • The updated video: sortie/<titre>.html, and the preview reloaded.
    • A copy of the previous state, in the video’s hidden .historique/ folder (the last 20, plus your named versions, 20 at most), with the sentence for each edit.
    • After a visual edit, a before / after sheet (sortie/planche/avant-apres-<scène>.png): the before image, the after image, and the differences in red.
    • Your notes and their images, in sortie/remarques/: « annule » (“undo”) doesn’t erase them.
    • An updated check report (sortie/controle.json). Whatever the check fixed on its own is told to you in one sentence.

    Risks and precautions

    • A precise length or spelling is respected. If you ask for « la scène 3 dure 4 secondes » (“scene 3 lasts 4 seconds”) or a particular spelling, Pulse Motion checks without fixing anything on its own, so as not to undo what you asked for. It only points out what causes a problem.
    • Changing the style rewrites some scenes. Ready-made scenes are copied over into the new style; freely composed scenes are rewritten by an animator. « annule » (“undo”) brings back the old style.
    • The music follows the picture. A changed length or transition doesn’t throw the composed music out of step: it is remade to the new timing, each scene with its own section. Music from a file, on the other hand, doesn’t move: with its tempo, each scene has to start on a beat, and Pulse Motion adjusts the lengths itself.

    Tip: one edit at a time. If you string three together in one sentence, « annule » (“undo”) will reverse all three at once.

    Problems and solutions

    « Rien à rétablir » (“Nothing to redo”)

    « rétablis » (“redo”) reverses the last undo. A new edit made since then erases what could have been redone: to get back to a precise state, save it under a name before trying something else.

    « Vous gardez déjà 20 versions nommées » (“You’re already keeping 20 named versions”)

    Say which one to forget (« oublie la version brouillon » (“forget the draft version”)), then save the new one.

    « Aucune retouche à annuler » (“No edit to undo”)

    You have gone back to the very first saved state, or the video has never been edited. There is nothing older to restore.

    The preview didn’t reload

    It may have been closed. Pulse Motion reopens it if it is no longer active; otherwise, reload the browser page (F5, or Cmd + R on Mac).

    The text is too long and still overflows

    The check shrinks overflowing text down to 80% of its size, never below what stays readable. Beyond that, it has to be shortened: « raccourcis le titre de la scène 2 » (“shorten the title of scene 2”). The maximum lengths for each style are in the catalogue.

    Pulse Motion tells me a word in the voice can’t be found

    An animation starts on a word of your voice, but that word is no longer said (you changed the text or re-recorded). Pulse Motion suggests another word in the scene as the starting point.
