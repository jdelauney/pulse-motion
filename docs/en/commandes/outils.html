Step 1 · Create

/pulse-motion:outils

Check that the computer is ready for videos and install what is missing (ffmpeg, transcription, Python), with your consent. Never creates or changes a video.

    Description

    Pulse Motion relies on a few programs on your computer. This command simply tells you what is ready and what is missing, then installs what is missing, with your consent. It works in any folder, with or without a video.

    It starts with the installed version of Pulse Motion (« Pulse Motion 0.16.0 est installé » (“Pulse Motion 0.16.0 is installed”)), then shows a table:

    ToolWhat it is for
    Chrome or EdgeWatching the video, checking it, exporting it.
    ffmpegA high-quality MP4 with clean sound. Without it, the export is done in the browser.
    PythonNeeded for transcription.
    TranscriptionAligning the subtitles and animations with the actual words of your voice. Optional.
    The computer’s voiceThe placeholder voice, before you record your own.
    The synthetic voiceA French voice generated on the computer, which can be delivered as it is. Optional, installed only if you ask for it.

    Each missing tool is followed by what you can do, for example: say « installe ffmpeg » (“install ffmpeg”).

    Why use it

    • You can see at a glance whether everything is ready, before a first export or a first recording.
    • Nothing is installed without your consent. Pulse Motion asks you « Installer (Recommandé) » (Install (Recommended)) or « Pas maintenant » (Not now), one installation at a time.
    • You have nothing to type on Windows and Mac: Pulse Motion runs the installation itself. On Linux, it gives you the line to type.
    • Your videos aren’t touched. The tools are installed on the computer; no video is created or changed.

    When to use it

    1. 1 Create

    • Before your first video, once the plugin is installed (see the requirements).
    • Before /pulse-motion:exporter, to install ffmpeg and get a high-fidelity export.
    • Before /pulse-motion:voix, to install transcription if you want to import a recording of the whole video or realign the words.
    • When a message mentions a missing tool.
    • When something isn’t working: « signale un problème » (“report a problem”). Claude prepares a summary (version, system, tools, latest errors), with nothing from your video, and saves it in a file. With your consent, it opens the reporting page, already filled in: you read it over and send it yourself (a GitHub account is needed; otherwise, send the file by email). Nothing is ever sent on your behalf.
    • When you change computer: « emporte mes chartes sur mon autre ordinateur » (“take my brand kits to my other computer”). Your personal library (brand kits, styles, effects, characters, preferences) goes into a single file, which you copy yourself.

    How to run it

    /pulse-motion:outils

    On its own, it checks the computer. To install a tool, say so right after the name or in the conversation.

    /pulse-motion:outils installe ffmpeg

    “install ffmpeg”

    /pulse-motion:outils installe la transcription

    “install transcription”

    What you can ask

    You sayWhat happens
    « vérifie mon ordinateur » (“check my computer”), « est-ce que tout est prêt pour l'export ? » (“is everything ready for export?”)The Pulse Motion version and the table of tools, ready or missing.
    « installe ffmpeg » (“install ffmpeg”)Pulse Motion explains in one sentence what it is for, asks for your consent, installs it, then checks that it is really there.
    « installe la transcription » (“install transcription”)Three choices: « Installer (Recommandé) » (Install (Recommended)), about 800 MB to download once; « Version légère » (Light version), 250 MB, slightly less accurate; « Pas maintenant » (Not now). If Python is missing, it is installed first, with your consent.
    « installe la voix de synthèse » (“install the synthetic voice”)Pulse Motion says in one sentence what it is (a single French voice, female, less natural than a real voice) and its size (about 300 MB, or 550 MB for the more faithful version), then asks for your consent. Nothing is downloaded without it. It needs Python, version 3.10 to 3.13.
    « emporte mes chartes sur mon autre ordinateur » (“take my brand kits to my other computer”), « emporte ma bibliothèque » (“take my library with me”)Pulse Motion tells you what your library contains, then offers « Tout emporter (Recommandé) » (Take everything (Recommended)) or « Choisir » (Choose). It turns it into a ZIP file, saved in your Documents, which you copy like any other (USB stick, email). Sign-ins to the sites you have filmed never leave.
    « importe ma bibliothèque » (“import my library”) (with the ZIP file)Pulse Motion first tells you what is new, what you already have, and what has a name that is already taken. Nothing already on the computer is replaced without your consent: for each taken name, « Garder le mien (Recommandé) » (Keep mine (Recommended)) or « Prendre celui de l'archive » (Take the one from the archive). The preferences it remembered for you are added to yours.
    « installe Python » (“install Python”)Only if it is missing or too old: Pulse Motion asks for your consent, installs it, then checks.

    The same tools, installed by hand, are described in the requirements.

    What it produces

    • An overview in plain words: what is ready, what is missing, and how to get it.
    • The tools installed on the computer, if you accept: ffmpeg, Python, transcription. Nothing in your video changes.
    • What to do next: the action that was waiting for the tool (the export, recording a voice), otherwise creating a video with /pulse-motion:creer.

    Risks and precautions

    • Transcription is a big download. About 800 MB (250 MB for the light version), just once. Installation takes a few minutes: Pulse Motion warns you and waits until it is finished.
    • A tool you have just installed may stay invisible. The computer only sees a new program when Claude Code restarts. Restart it, then ask « vérifie mon ordinateur » (“check my computer”) again.
    • On Linux, you type the installation line yourself, in a terminal, because it asks for your password. Pulse Motion waits for you to tell it you’re done, then checks.
    • The browser and the microphone can’t be installed from here. Chrome or Edge are installed from their website. The microphone is tested in the voice studio: ask « j'enregistre la scène 1 » (“I’m recording scene 1”) when a video is open.

    Tip: ffmpeg and transcription are optional. Without ffmpeg, the export is done in the browser; without transcription, the words of the voice are aligned as well as possible. Everything else works.

    Problems and solutions

    ffmpeg is still missing after installation

    The computer only sees it when Claude Code restarts. Close Claude Code, start it again, then ask « vérifie mon ordinateur » (“check my computer”) again.

    Python still can’t be found

    Same cause: restart Claude Code to finish the installation, then ask « installe la transcription » (“install transcription”) again.

    « Python est trop ancien » (“Python is too old”)

    Transcription needs a more recent version. Ask « installe Python » (“install Python”): Pulse Motion installs the version required, with your consent.

    Installing transcription failed

    Pulse Motion tells you why, in plain words. Everything works without it: the words of the voice are aligned as well as possible. You can try again later.

    Chrome or Edge are reported missing

    Install one of the two from its website, then ask « vérifie mon ordinateur » (“check my computer”) again. See Chrome or Edge.

    « Ce nom est déjà pris » (“This name is already taken”) when importing the library

    On this computer, you already have a brand kit, a style, an effect or a character with the same name, but different. Nothing has been replaced: choose whether to keep yours or take the one from the archive. The rest of the library is imported.

    “node” not found

    Node.js isn’t installed: Pulse Motion can’t check anything without it. Install the LTS version from nodejs.org (installer), then restart Claude Code. See the requirements.
