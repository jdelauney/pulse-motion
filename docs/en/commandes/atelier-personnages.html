Step 4 · Customise

/pulse-motion:atelier-personnages

Open the character workshop to pose and animate a character with the mouse (« je veux régler son salut moi-même » (“I want to adjust his wave myself”), « laisse-moi placer ses bras » (“let me place his arms”)).

    Description

    The character workshop is an editor that opens in your browser. Pulse Motion opens it on the pose or move you ask for (“his wave” is the coucou move), with your character: the video’s character if there is only one, otherwise it asks you which. Without a drawn character, it is the stick figure.

    In the workshop:

    • Dragging a joint rotates the limb.
    • Shift + dragging a hand or a foot brings it under the mouse: the arm or leg follows.
    • The strip at the bottom holds the moments of the move: you adjust the character at each moment.
    • « Enregistrer » (Save) stores the result with your video; « annule » (“undo”) reverses it.

    The workshop is also used to fix a cut-out, for a character taken from your illustration with /pulse-motion:decouper-personnage: click a shape then a bone of the skeleton to assign it, drag the dots to move the joints, « Découper » (Cut out) to see the walking result, and « Enregistrer ».

    When you say you have finished, Pulse Motion reads what you saved, looks at the move’s sheet and describes it to you in one sentence.

    Why use it

    • Some gestures are easier to show than to describe. Placing a hand in an exact spot, setting the angle of an elbow: it is simpler with the mouse.
    • You see the result as you work, from every angle.
    • You risk nothing. Until you have saved, nothing changes; afterwards, « annule » (“undo”) goes back.
    • A cut-out can be fixed without starting over. A misplaced elbow moves with one gesture.

    How to run it

    /pulse-motion:atelier-personnages [le mouvement ou la pose à ouvrir, et le personnage]

    “[the move or pose to open, and the character]”

    In square brackets [ ]: optional. You can also run the command on its own and then say what you want to adjust.

    /pulse-motion:atelier-personnages je veux régler son salut moi-même

    “I want to adjust his wave myself”

    /pulse-motion:atelier-personnages je veux voir le cuisinier sous tous les angles

    “I want to see the chef from every angle”

    /pulse-motion:atelier-personnages laisse-moi corriger la découpe

    “let me fix the cut-out”

    You only say « ouvre l'éditeur » (“open the editor”), and your video also has particle effects? Pulse Motion asks you which: « Les personnages (Recommandé) » (The characters, Recommended) or « Les effets de particules » (The particle effects). For effects, use /pulse-motion:atelier-particules.

    What it produces

    • The workshop open in your browser, on the requested move or pose.
    • What you save, stored with the video: the adjusted pose or move, or the fixed cut-out. If you saved it under a new name, Pulse Motion tells you.
    • A sheet of the result, once you have finished, to see it in motion again.

    Risks and precautions

    • Without a video in the folder, nothing is saved. The workshop opens anyway, to look and try things out; Pulse Motion warns you.
    • Tell Claude when you have finished. That is when Pulse Motion reads what you saved and shows you the result.
    • A save can be undone. « annule » (“undo”) goes back to the previous state, as for any edit.

    Tip: start with the most telling moment of the gesture (the arm at the very top of a wave), save, look, then refine the other moments on the strip.

    Problems and solutions

    I clicked “Enregistrer” (Save) and nothing changed in my video

    The open folder probably has no video in it: the workshop adjusts, but does not save. Open your video’s folder in VS Code and run the command again.

    Pulse Motion asks me which editor to open

    Your video has characters and particle effects, and “the editor” could mean either. Choose « Les personnages » (The characters); for effects, use /pulse-motion:atelier-particules.

    A limb turns around the wrong point

    The joint is in the wrong place in the cut-out. Ask « laisse-moi corriger la découpe » (“let me fix the cut-out”), then drag the joint’s dot (the elbow, the knee) to its centre, and « Enregistrer ».

    I want to go back to the previous version

    Say « annule » (“undo”), for example with /pulse-motion:retoucher annule: your save is undone.

    “node” not found

    Node.js is not installed. Install the LTS version from nodejs.org, then restart Claude Code. See the requirements.

    Examples

    /pulse-motion:atelier-personnages je veux régler son salut moi-même

    pulse-motion personnages liste --dossier .

    pulse-motion personnages atelier coucou --dessin cuisinier --dossier .

    L'atelier des personnages est ouvert sur le salut du cuisinier. Glissez une articulation pour tourner le membre ; Maj + glisser une main ou un pied l'amène sous la souris. La frise du bas porte les moments du salut : « Enregistrer » range le résultat avec la vidéo, et « annule » le défait.

    J'ai fini.

    pulse-motion personnages planche coucou --dessin cuisinier --dossier .

    Votre salut est enregistré : le bras monte plus haut et la main reste ouverte. Voici la planche.

    In English

    You: /pulse-motion:atelier-personnages I want to adjust his wave myself

    Claude: The character workshop is open on the chef’s wave. Drag a joint to rotate the limb; Shift + drag a hand or a foot brings it under the mouse. The strip at the bottom holds the moments of the wave: “Enregistrer” (Save) stores the result with the video, and “annule” (undo) reverses it.

    You: I’ve finished.

    Claude: Your wave is saved: the arm goes up higher and the hand stays open. Here is the sheet.

    /pulse-motion:atelier-personnages le coude de la mascotte est mal placé

    pulse-motion personnages atelier --decoupe mascotte --dossier .

    L'atelier montre votre illustration découpée. Cliquez une forme puis un os pour l'y ranger, glissez les ronds pour déplacer les articulations. « Découper » montre le résultat qui marche ; « Enregistrer » le range.

    C'est bon.

    pulse-motion personnages planche marche --dessin mascotte --dossier .

    Le bras de la mascotte plie maintenant au bon endroit. Voici sa marche.

    In English

    You: /pulse-motion:atelier-personnages the mascot’s elbow is in the wrong place

    Claude: The workshop shows your cut-out illustration. Click a shape then a bone to assign it, drag the dots to move the joints. “Découper” (Cut out) shows the walking result; “Enregistrer” (Save) stores it.

    You: That’s fine.

    Claude: The mascot’s arm now bends in the right place. Here is its walk.
