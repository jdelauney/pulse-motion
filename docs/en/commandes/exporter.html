Step 3 · Export

/pulse-motion:exporter

Export the video as an MP4 set up for its use (the web, social media, a meeting, a video editor, an email), made on your computer, ready to publish or send.

    Description

    The animated page is watched in a browser; the MP4 file can be published anywhere: social media, a training platform, email, YouTube. This command makes it on your computer, without sending anything online.

    It takes the video as it is: it changes nothing. You say what the video is for (“for LinkedIn”, “to send by email”) and Pulse Motion picks the settings: the picture size, its sharpness, the file size. It checks the video first, then chooses the best way to export:

    ModeWhenWhat you get
    High-fidelity exportffmpeg is installed (Pulse Motion offers it to you once)The browser’s real frames, one by one, and the sound at the right level, checked. The time depends on the use (see the table below).
    In-browser exportWithout ffmpeg, or if you decline to install itAn MP4 made directly in Chrome or Edge, with nothing to install. A few advanced effects are rendered less well.

    Both exports have exactly the same sound as the preview: the music, the voice and the sound effects are mixed only once, in the page, and brought to the level the streaming platforms expect. The video’s colours are the page’s, in common players (Chrome, Safari, VLC, PowerPoint).

    After a first export, another use redoes no frames: it starts again from the frames already captured. The email version aims for under 10 MB (or the size you say: « moins de 25 Mo pour Teams » (“under 25 MB for Teams”)); if the video is too long to fit, Pulse Motion tells you before starting, with the size it would need. The working version is made quickly, by the graphics card when it can: it is for checking, never for publishing.

    In the preview, the MP4 button in the playback bar offers four uses: Web, Réseaux (Social media), Présentation (Presentation), Courriel (Email).

    Chapters are only included if you asked for them (« ajoute des chapitres » (“add chapters”), an edit); they are then also built into the MP4 when ffmpeg is installed, and YouTube shows them from three upwards. Credits come with every export: fonts, music, voice, images and sounds, with their licence. A file you imported without saying where it came from is marked « à préciser » (to be specified): just say it (« la musique vient de Pixabay » (“the music comes from Pixabay”)).

    The master adds a light motion blur to fast passages, like a camera (never on text to be read): the export takes longer where things move fast (30% longer for the one-minute video, more than twice as long for the very lively three-minute one). The no-background video refuses a scene with a solid background, and names it: Pulse Motion offers to remove that background for this version. For a video that loops (« fais-la tourner en boucle » (“make it loop”), an edit), the music no longer fades in or out, and the check makes sure the end joins the beginning without a jolt.

    A scene, a passage or a GIF start from the frames already made: after an export, they capture nothing again. They never touch the full video. For a vertical video made for TikTok, Shorts or Reels, say so (an edit): the check then makes sure nothing sits under the app’s buttons, and the subtitles move above them. In the preview, the Z key shows this area.

    A page that can make its own MP4. At the end of the export, Claude also offers you a page that makes its MP4 by itself, even without Claude (about 250 KB more); the recommended answer is « Non merci » (No thanks). With this page, open it later in Chrome or Edge and click MP4 in the playback bar: the video is made in the browser, then you choose where to save it. In another browser, the page tells you to open it in Chrome or Edge. For the best quality, exporting through Claude is still recommended. « Une page plus légère, sans le bouton MP4 » (“A lighter page, without the MP4 button”) puts it back as before.

    Why use it

    • Everything stays with you. No online service, no queue, no watermark.
    • What you see is what you get. The high-fidelity export films the browser frame by frame: no stutter, even on a modest computer.
    • Clean sound. The sound level is checked at export; the music lowers itself under the voice.
    • An interrupted export is not lost. The parts already done are picked up at the next export.

    How to run it

    /pulse-motion:exporter

    It takes nothing after the name, but you can say what you want:

    You sayPulse Motion exports
    (nothing)It asks you what the video is for: the web (recommended), social media, an email or a meeting. A vertical video, or one made for social media, suggests social media first.
    « pour YouTube », « pour mon site », « pour notre plateforme de formation »(“for YouTube”, “for my website”, “for our training platform”) For the web: the preview’s quality.
    « pour LinkedIn », « pour Instagram »(“for LinkedIn”, “for Instagram”) For social media: a little sharper, to stand up to the platforms’ recompression.
    « pour la projeter », « pour PowerPoint »(“to project it”, “for PowerPoint”) For presentation: very sharp, plays in PowerPoint and Keynote.
    « pour mon monteur », « la meilleure qualité »(“for my video editor”, “the best quality”) The master: the most faithful file, to rework, with a light motion blur on fast passages (like a camera); « sans flou » (“no blur”) removes it.
    « à incruster dans mon montage », « fond transparent »(“to overlay in my edit”, “transparent background”) A video with no background, to place over another picture in editing software (see the table above).
    « par courriel », « moins de 20 Mo »(“by email”, “under 20 MB”) A light version that stays under the stated size (10 MB if you don’t say).
    « vite fait, juste pour vérifier »(“quickly, just to check”) A working version, small and fast, not for publishing.
    « plus fluide »(“smoother”) At 60 frames per second. The export takes twice as long.
    « une version de travail avec la voix de l'ordinateur »(“a working version with the computer’s voice”) With the placeholder voice, only if you ask for it.
    « juste la scène 3 », « en GIF », « dans un téléphone », « un PDF des scènes », « en boucle »(“just scene 3”, “as a GIF”, “in a phone”, “a PDF of the scenes”, “looping”) The other outputs (see the table above).
    « un texte pour accompagner la vidéo »(“a text to go with the video”) Pulse Motion writes sortie/<titre>-textes.md; it offers it to you by itself after an export for social media or email.

    Before starting, Pulse Motion tells you how long it will take; during the export, it tracks the time remaining.

    FileWhat it is for

    sortie/<titre>-<usage>.mp4The other uses, each with its own file: -reseaux, -presentation, -master, -courriel, -brouillon.

    Pulse Motion finishes with a summary: the use, the length, the file size, the sound (or « muette », silent), the export mode, and each file with its role.

    Risks and precautions

    • Errors come first. If errors remain, Pulse Motion tells you plainly and offers to fix them first with /pulse-motion:retoucher. Simple warnings do not block the export: it mentions them in one sentence.
    • The placeholder voice is never published by mistake. If a scene still speaks with the computer’s voice, Pulse Motion offers to record your voice first. If you choose « une version de travail » (a working version), it does not ask again as long as that voice doesn’t change, and the file is called <titre>-version-de-travail.mp4: the final video is not touched.
    • ffmpeg is only installed with your agreement, and only with your system’s official command (winget on Windows, Homebrew on macOS).
    • No incomplete files. If the export fails, no half-written MP4 is left in sortie/. An email version that would exceed the stated size is never written.
    • A scene with an error stops the export. If a scene’s script fails during the export, Pulse Motion stops and tells you which one, and at roughly what moment.

    Warning: a flash more than three times per second over a large part of the picture is an error, not a warning: it can trigger an epileptic seizure. Pulse Motion offers to soften it before exporting.

    Problems and solutions

    “Chrome ou Edge introuvable” (Chrome or Edge not found)

    The export needs one of the two. Install Chrome or Edge (see the requirements). Another option: open the preview in one of them and click the MP4 button in the playback bar.

    The video is exported silent, or without the music

    A sound file could not be read. Pulse Motion tells you in a warning: the video is exported without the damaged source. Give the file again (music or voice), then run the export again.

    The export was done “in the browser” when I wanted the high-fidelity export

    ffmpeg was not installed, or its version is too old. Pulse Motion offers to install it for next time. See ffmpeg.

    The export stopped halfway

    Just run /pulse-motion:exporter again: the parts already done are picked up.

    The file is too big for an email

    Ask for « une version pour l'envoyer par courriel » (“a version to send by email”): it stays under 10 MB, or under the size you say. If the video is too long, Pulse Motion suggests a larger size or a single scene.

    “Un export de cette vidéo est déjà en cours” (An export of this video is already running)

    Another export of the same video is still running (the message says since what time): wait for it to finish, then run it again.
