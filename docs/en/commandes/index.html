The commands

You type each command in Claude Code, starting with /pulse-motion:. Then you answer the questions in your own words: Pulse Motion runs everything else itself and never shows you any code. Not sure which one? Quelle commande pour… ? (in French) guides you in two or three questions.

Step 1 Create

  • /pulse-motion:creerCreate a motion design video in a few questions, approve its timeline, then watch it in your browser. Music, voice and subtitles optional.
  • /pulse-motion:filmerFilm a web app for a tutorial, step by step, or start from a recording of your screen: pointer, clicks, zooms and spotlight added afterwards, personal data blurred.
  • /pulse-motion:reprendrePick up the current video where it stopped: a timeline to approve, scenes to write, a preview to reopen.
  • /pulse-motion:outilsCheck that the computer is ready and install what is missing, with your consent: ffmpeg for export, transcription, Python. Never touches a video.

Step 2 Edit

  • /pulse-motion:retoucherEdit the video in one sentence (« ralentis la scène 3 » (“slow down scene 3”), « change le titre » (“change the title”), « baisse la musique » (“turn the music down”), « annule » (“undo”)) and see it straight away in the preview.
  • /pulse-motion:voixRecord your voice in the preview’s studio, import a recording, add a placeholder voice, realign the words, or play the music on its own.

Step 3 Export

  • /pulse-motion:exporterExport the video as an MP4 tuned for its use (web, social media, presentation, master, email, draft), made on your computer, ready to publish or send.

Step 4 Customise

  • /pulse-motion:styleCreate your own video style (« un style papercraft aux couleurs pop-art » (“a papercraft style in pop-art colours”)): look, colours, fonts and moves, checked before use.
  • /pulse-motion:dessiner-personnageDraw a character for the video (« un cuisinier rondouillard avec une toque » (“a chubby chef in a toque”)), or edit its drawing, and see it move.
  • /pulse-motion:animer-personnageCreate or change a pose, a move or an expression, and see it from several angles. Put a character in a scene and make it talk.
  • /pulse-motion:decouper-personnageAnimate a character illustration you provide (Illustrator, Inkscape, Figma…) by cutting it up onto the Pulse Motion skeleton.
  • /pulse-motion:atelier-personnagesOpen the character workshop to pose and animate a character with the mouse, or fix how an illustration was cut up.
  • /pulse-motion:atelier-particulesOpen the particle effects workshop to tune an effect while you watch it: sparks, snow, confetti…
