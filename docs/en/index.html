The motion design plugin for Claude Code

Your idea as an animated video, in minutes.

You describe the video in your own words. Pulse Motion writes the timeline, asks you to approve it, animates each scene, checks readability on its own, then opens the video in your browser. An edit takes one sentence. Everything is made on your computer, with no cloud and no editing software.

Pulse Motion is made for French speakers: the plugin’s questions, messages and reports are in French, and Claude answers in French while it uses the plugin. This English guide helps you discover it; the requests shown as examples stay in French, with their translation alongside.

Why Pulse Motion

Publish more often, with videos that feel like you

An animated video holds attention far better than text. Yet few people publish them, nobody edits them on their own, and a consistent series of videos seems out of reach. Today there are three ways to go about it, and each one gets stuck somewhere.

  • An online template toolFast. But the videos all look alike, and you can’t control the pace.
  • A freelance video editorReal expertise. But it’s expensive, it takes time, and every edit costs a round trip.
  • Professional animation softwareAnything is possible. But it takes months to learn, and most people give up after a few evenings of tutorials.

What Pulse Motion changes

  • Your words are enough. You describe the video as you would to a colleague; Pulse Motion writes the timeline, you approve it, and it animates.
  • It knows the craft. Reading pace, typography, contrast, sound at the right level: checked every time, and fixed on its own when it’s safe.
  • A video that feels like you. A wide range of styles, your logo, your colours and fonts, your voice: no two videos look the same.
  • The edits are yours. « Ralentis la scène 3 » (“Slow down scene 3”): you see the result straight away, whether it’s the night before your session or five minutes before you publish.
  • Everything is made at home. On your computer, with no cloud and no editing software; the video belongs to you.

What it can do

A polished video, with nothing to learn

Pulse Motion takes care of the craft: reading pace, French typography, contrast, sound at the right level. You stay in charge of what matters: the message.

  • Twenty styles, your coloursClear and educational, warm, editorial, energetic, minimal, dark tech, silent info, technical blueprint, launch, whiteboard, blackboard, notebook, data story, isometric infographic, isometric album, bold crayon, paper cut-out, riso, pictograms, 1950s instruction manual. Your logo, colours and fonts apply to all of them.
  • Sound includedMusic composed on your computer, your voice recorded in the preview, sound effects and subtitles timed to the words. (in French)
  • Checks that fix thingsText that overflows, a scene too short to read, dead time, typography: the safe cases are fixed on their own, and Pulse Motion tells you so.
  • One edit, one sentence« Ralentis la scène 3 » (“slow down scene 3”), « mets mes couleurs » (“use my colours”), « annule » (“undo”): the preview reloads straight away, at the same point.
  • The name of every movePop-in, mask reveal, whip pan, zoom-through… 128 moves from the trade, each one shown in motion, with the sentence to ask for it.
  • Particle effectsSparks on a key figure, confetti for the conclusion, snow or smoke for atmosphere: thirty-four effects, used with restraint, to watch in motion.
  • Animated charactersA character drawn on request, or your own mascot: it walks in, points to a figure, smiles, waves, and speaks with your voice. (in French)
  • Two deliverablesAn MP4 file to publish, and an animated page that opens offline, ready to put on your website.

Install and update

Pulse Motion in two commands

Once Claude Code and Node.js are installed (see the requirements), type these commands in Claude Code, one at a time.

Install

/plugin marketplace add jdelauney/pulse-motion
/plugin install pulse-motion@pulse-motion

Close and restart Claude Code: the /pulse-motion:… commands appear.

Update

From time to time, in a terminal:

claude plugin marketplace update pulse-motion
claude plugin update pulse-motion@pulse-motion

Close and restart Claude Code: the new version loads at startup.

Tip: to stop thinking about it, type /plugin in Claude Code, open Marketplaces, choose pulse-motion, then Enable auto-update. The details are in the requirements.
