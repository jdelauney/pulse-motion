Start from an idea

Video ideas

Nine kinds of video that Pulse Motion knows by heart. For each one: a finished example, sentences to copy as they are, and the structure it will follow. Copy a sentence, change the topic, and off you go.

How to use it

  1. Watch the example that looks like your project.
  2. Copy a sentence and replace the topic with your own.
  3. Give it to Claude Code: /pulse-motion:creer recognises the video template, tells you so in one sentence, then asks you its usual questions. Everything can still be changed.

Want to see the result right there on your computer? In an empty folder, run the command below: the finished example is copied into it and opens in the preview, ready to be edited.

Short training video

Trainers, quality or safety managers, teachers: an instruction people need to remember.

One minute to get a rule or a gesture across: why it matters, the essentials, what to do and what to avoid. 1 min or so, landscape (16:9).

Sentences to copy

“a one-minute training video on the cold chain”

“a short video explaining waste sorting at the office”

/pulse-motion:creer un rappel des gestes de sécurité avant d'utiliser une échelle

“a reminder of the safety steps before using a ladder”

For your audience
