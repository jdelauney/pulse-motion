Quick start

One sentence, a few answers, and you’re watching an animated video of about one minute in your browser, ready to export as an MP4. Five minutes of your time, plus a few minutes of building during which you have nothing to do.

Pulse Motion is made for French speakers: the plugin’s questions, messages and reports are in French, and Claude answers in French while it uses the plugin. This English guide helps you discover it; the requests shown as examples stay in French, with their translation alongside.

0 Before you start 1 minute

You need Claude Code, Node.js, Chrome or Edge, and the Pulse Motion plugin. If any of the four is missing, the requirements page installs them step by step; then come back here.

Open an empty folder in VS Code (the video will be stored there), then ask Claude to check your computer:

/pulse-motion:outils

What you should see: Pulse Motion shows a table of what’s ready. Node.js and the browser must be ticked; ❌ lines for ffmpeg, Python or transcription don’t stop you from starting. If anything is missing, it offers to install everything in a single question: « Tout installer (Recommandé) » (Install everything (Recommended)). One approval, and no need to restart Claude Code.

1 Create 2 minutes

Describe your video in one sentence, after the command:

/pulse-motion:creer la chaîne du froid pour mes stagiaires

“the cold chain, for my trainees”

Pulse Motion asks two short rounds of questions (length, audience, music, voice and subtitles). To go fast, each time keep the answer marked (Recommandé) (Recommended); for the audience, choose the one your video is for; at « À ajouter » (To add), answer « Rien pour l’instant » (Nothing for now); for the voice, « Pas de voix » (No voice). Even quicker: when asked about the path, choose « Faites pour moi » (Do it for me), and Pulse Motion won’t ask you any more questions. It then shows you the timeline, the list of scenes with their text, and three possible styles.

Voici le déroulé : 6 scènes, environ 1 minute. → Je valide, avec le style posé, lisible et rassurant (Recommandé) / … / Je veux changer quelque chose

Je valide, avec le style posé, lisible et rassurant

In English

Claude: Here is the timeline: 6 scenes, about 1 minute. → I approve, with the calm, clear and reassuring style (Recommended) / … / I want to change something

You: I approve, with the calm, clear and reassuring style

What you should see: a first animated draft opens in your browser in under a minute. Pulse Motion then animates the scenes, which replace the draft one by one, and checks readability on its own: allow a few minutes, there’s nothing for you to do.

2 Watch 1 minute

The preview has been open in your browser since the draft: it’s the video, with a playback bar at the bottom. Click ▶ or press the Space bar.

Something you don’t like? Say it in one sentence, and the preview reloads straight away:

/pulse-motion:retoucher ralentis la scène 3

“slow down scene 3”

3 Export 1 minute

/pulse-motion:exporter

Pulse Motion makes the MP4 file on your computer, in a few minutes for a one-minute video.

What you should see: the sortie/ folder contains the MP4, ready to publish or send, and the animated page, which opens offline.
