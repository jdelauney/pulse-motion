The move dictionary

Moves

The name of each move, so you can ask Claude for it: « un pop-in sur le titre » (“a pop-in on the title”), « un whip pan entre la scène 2 et la 3 » (“a whip pan between scenes 2 and 3”), or simply its number: « #001 ». Every preview is rendered by Pulse Motion itself.

How to use it

  1. Watch: the previews play when they are on screen.
  2. Open a move’s card and copy the sentence to ask for it.
  3. Give it to /pulse-motion:retoucher on a video in progress, or slip it into your request to /pulse-motion:creer.

On your computer, you can also choose by watching: say « montre-moi les mouvements » (“show me the moves”), tick the ones you like in the page that opens, then click « Envoyer à Claude » (Send to Claude).
